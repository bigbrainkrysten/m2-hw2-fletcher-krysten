Introduction

My first love has always been music. It has been a safe place for me to explore who I am and what I am feeling. From the time I was little, when my parents showed me different genre's I realized I had something that allowed me to connect with my emotions before I even knew what they were. In many ways, music saved me from the world I was in. It was in every way, an escape. It did not matter what was happening around me, I could put my headphones in and go somewhere else.

My favorite artist is Mac Miller, and one of my favorite albums to ever exist is 'The Divine Feminine'. I had been listening to Mac since I was 13. I was devastated when he passed. I'm not usually one to get attached to artist but he was something special. Very parasocial of me, but it was like losing one of your childhood friends.The day he passed was first day working at Apple. I actually had tickets to see him for the opening night of the Swimming Tour. I had meet and greet tickets; he passed 3 weeks before. He is arguably one of the most influential people in music. Everyone loves him. Here is a link to his album: The Divine Feminine
